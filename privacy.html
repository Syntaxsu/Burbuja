Burbuja Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Burbuja is a Spanish vocabulary game. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing.

Information we collect

None. Burbuja does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Internet access

The app does not request internet permission. It works fully offline, and nothing you do in the game leaves your phone.

What stays on your phone

The app saves a few settings on your own device so they are there next time you play:

  • Whether sound is on or off
  • Which language you speak
  • Your best scores and how many rounds you have played

This stays on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Pronunciation

When the game speaks a word aloud, it uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on your phone. Burbuja only passes it the word to say. How that engine handles speech is covered by its provider's own privacy policy.

Children

Burbuja collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
